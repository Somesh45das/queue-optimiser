## 💡 Decision Framework

### When to Build a Feature?

**YES, Build Now** if:
- ✅ High user demand (>50% want it)
- ✅ Solves major pain point
- ✅ Quick to implement (<4 weeks)
- ✅ High ROI potential
- ✅ Competitive necessity

**MAYBE, Build Later** if:
- ⚠️ Medium demand (20-50%)
- ⚠️ Nice to have feature
- ⚠️ Moderate complexity (4-8 weeks)
- ⚠️ Uncertain ROI
- ⚠️ Differentiator but not critical

**NO, Don't Build** if:
- ❌ Low demand (<20%)
- ❌ High complexity (>12 weeks)
- ❌ Low impact
- ❌ Negative ROI
- ❌ Better alternatives exist
